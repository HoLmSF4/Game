How to Play

  • Click free land to found your realm, then click unclaimed land to expand. Every order raises an army that pushes toward the spot you clicked; the slider sets how many troops go.
  • Your armies are listed on the left. Orders to the same front reinforce each other and armies that meet merge. Press × to recall one: 1–5% are lost if it was fighting a realm, none against unclaimed land.
  • Click another realm for diplomacy. You must declare war before you attack; right-click attacks an enemy at once. Ask for peace, an alliance (3 minutes, renewable in its last minute), a trade agreement, send a gift or demand 20% of their gold.
  • Land your enemy has cut off is annexed when you surround it. The Shield meets every attack on you with an equal force.
  • Terrain matters: plains ×1, forest ×1.5, mountains ×2.5. Your Attack, Defence and Expansion speed start at 1.00 and rise with policies, courtiers, castles and events.
  • City (10 levels): troops and income. Castle (5): a fortified zone that grows. Port (5): merchant ships and fleets. Catapult (5): click it, press Fire and aim. Its stones burn any land they hit, your own included, and bombarding a realm means war. Click any of your buildings to upgrade it.
  • Crossing the sea needs a port and a Cog. War Galleys and Great Carracks fight: click one, then click open water to send it there.
  • Income arrives every 5 seconds. Taxes are thin; gold comes from merchant ships, trade agreements, allies, tribute and sacked capitals ★.
  • Stability rises when you conquer realms and falls when you lose land, cities, castles or ships; events move it too. It scales recruitment, and below 15 your troops desert. Realm Policy and the Court (three courtiers) shape your realm.
  • Hold 90% of the land to win. There is no time limit. Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom. Keys: 1–5 build, P policy, C court, N navy, S shield, Esc settings.
